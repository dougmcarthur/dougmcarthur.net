<p>My single "Magic" was named Song of the Week by Manitoba Music and picked up rotation on Power 97's River City Rocks and Made in Manitoba programs.</p>

<p>We launched it with a One-Day Choir event at The Forks, where members of the public learned and sang a bespoke choral arrangement of the song by local artist McKye, then played a release show that night at The Handsome Daughter.</p>
