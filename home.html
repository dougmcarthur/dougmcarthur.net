Bio

Doug McArthur, a prairie-born singer-songwriter, has always been driven by an insatiable desire to write and perform music. His voice captivates audiences, and his hooks linger in their minds long after the music stops.

As frontman and creative force behind rock bands Broken Halo and Soapbox, he Doug wrote and released two albums and an EP, played big rock festivals like Stage 13, and enjoyed regular rotation on rock radio.

A viral cover of “Pinball Wizard” featuring Doug on vocals caught the attention of The Who. He was invited to sing backing vocals live with rock legends Heart and has shared the stage with The Trews, The Tea Party, Sum 41, ZZ Top, and more. His band Broken Halo won the Power 97 Class of 2010 with their single “Rooftops.”

Now, Doug performs as a solo act. In 2023, he released his debut solo single, “Hermit Phase,” produced by Rusty Robot (Waking Eyes, Imaginary Cities). His follow-up single, “Draw The Line,” was released in 2024. Next, he released an EP in 2024, featuring a remix of “Draw The Line” produced by Lana Winterhalt.

For his most recent single, Magic, Doug organized something special - a One-Day Choir event with a bespoke choral arrangement composed by local artist McKye.

Magic was featured as Song of the Week on Manitoba Music, and has been featured on Power 97's River City Rocks show and on popular podcasts Witchpolice Radio and Made in Manitoba.

On stage, Doug also fronts Radio Riders, a five-piece band mixing crowd-favourite covers with his original songs.

Community

Through Sun Dogs Music (Sun Dogs Music Manitoba), Doug puts on a songwriter showcase every couple of months at The Handsome Daughter in Winnipeg, a listening room set up more like a workshop than a concert, and hosts small writing retreats out on Lake of the Woods a few times a year. He's also building Scout, a tool for researching and tracking opportunities for artists.
