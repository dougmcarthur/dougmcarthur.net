<p>On June 22, 2014, I sang live backing vocals for Heart at MTS Centre in Winnipeg (now Canada Life Centre), as part of a gospel choir drawn from the World Village Gospel Choir. We joined them for their closing number, Led Zeppelin's "Stairway to Heaven," with Jason Bonham on drums.</p>

<p>Sharing an arena stage with Ann and Nancy Wilson is one of the highlights of my performing life, full stop.</p>
