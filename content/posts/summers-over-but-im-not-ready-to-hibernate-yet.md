<p>Here on the prairies, it's getting a little colder every day and the leaves are begrudgingly changing colours. Trees show such a beautiful display of resistance to winter before they let all their leaves fall away. And I'm feeling that resistance a little, too. Even though I can feel the seasons changing, and I thought I'd be hunkering down to focus on writing and recording, I'm still barreling ahead with live performances. I'm playing three very different gigs this weekend!</p>



<p>Tonight (September 18), my cover band is playing a notorious dive bar called the East Gate Inn. My aim is to play a great show and avoid being stabbed. Just kidding, just kidding, just kidding, just kidding. </p>



<p>Tomorrow (September 19), I'm playing a <strong>sold-out</strong> solo gig at the <a href="https://tickets.pdcrailway.com/eventperformances.asp?evt=760" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Prairie Dog Central Railway's fall supper</a> (sadly not performing <em>on</em> the train - maybe next time). </p>



<p>And Sunday (September 20) is going to be extra special. I organized a show for my pal <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wQyyvm4-bAU&amp;t=1s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Von Bieker</a>, who is coming in from Edmonton for a weekend of shows &amp; showcases. Von Bieker and I will be joined by my new pal <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HhyII0L0lRo" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Shannon Chapman</a>, who recently moved back to Winnipeg after living and performing in Toronto for a long time. Shannon is a prolific and skilled songwriter with smooth, nimble vocals and an incendiary mastery on the keys, blending neo-soul and pop hooks with incisive and witty lyrics. We're performing at a small venue in Winnipeg called The Clubhouse, operated by Good &amp; Plenty Arts Collective. At The Clubhouse, the seating is couches and loveseats - it's so cozy! It's a small room with limited capacity, so <a href="https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/von-bieker-w-shannon-chapman-doug-mcarthur-tickets-2000041261252" data-type="link" data-id="https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/von-bieker-w-shannon-chapman-doug-mcarthur-tickets-2000041261252">grab your tickets while you can</a>. It's pay-what-you-can, with a suggested minimum of $10. </p>
