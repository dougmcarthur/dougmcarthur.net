<p>I'm excited to share my latest with you - check out two new demos, "<a href="https://soundcloud.com/dougmcarthur/magic-may-8-2024">Magic</a>" and "<a href="https://soundcloud.com/dougmcarthur/lost-weekends-may-24-demo">Lost Weekends</a>." In the "Lost Weekends" demo, I tried out the new Session Players feature in Logic Pro 11 to add virtual drums, bass, and piano to the track. It required a bit of fine-tuning to make the virtual players align with my arrangement, but the end result is pretty good.
